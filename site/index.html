How it works

From conversation to notes.

  1. Choose to record.Gappd captures microphone and system audio with macOS permissions.
  2. Follow the transcript.Apple Speech creates a live transcript while the meeting runs.
  3. Review private notes.The default local model produces a summary and stores it in your meeting history.
Clear privacy boundaries

Your meeting data is not an advertising product.

Gappd does not include advertising or behavioral analytics. An optional Installed Codex provider can send transcript text through your Codex setup only when you select it.
