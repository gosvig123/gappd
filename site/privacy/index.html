Gappd privacy policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Operator: Kristian Gosvig

Gappd is local-first. The default configuration processes and stores meeting content on your Mac. The current public version does not request Google Calendar access.

1. Who operates Gappd

Gappd is operated by Kristian Gosvig. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to leasemobil@gmail.com.

2. Data processed on your Mac

When you choose to record a meeting, Gappd can process microphone audio, system audio, transcript text, speaker information, meeting titles, summaries, and action items. It also stores app settings and local model configuration.

The default Local AI provider processes transcript text on your Mac. Meeting content and settings are stored in local app files and a local SQLite database.

3. Optional Installed Codex provider

If you explicitly select Installed Codex as the summary provider, Gappd sends transcript text through the Codex command-line tool and account already configured on your Mac. Recorded audio remains local. Processing by that provider is governed by your provider agreement and configuration.

4. macOS permissions

Gappd requests microphone, screen and system audio recording, and speech recognition permissions to provide recording and transcription. You control these permissions in macOS System Settings and can revoke them at any time.

5. Network connections

Gappd can use the network to check for and download app updates, download local model files, open support links, and use an optional provider that you configure. The current app does not include advertising or behavioral analytics.

6. Google Calendar status

Google Calendar is not available in the current public version. Gappd does not currently request access to or process Google Calendar data. Before a Calendar integration is released, this policy and the in-app disclosure will be updated to explain the exact data accessed, purpose, storage, sharing, retention, and deletion behavior.

7. Sharing and sale

Gappd does not sell meeting data or use it for advertising. Gappd does not share meeting content with the operator in the default local configuration. Data can reach a third-party provider only when you choose and configure that provider, or when disclosure is required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

Local meeting data remains on your Mac until you delete the meeting or remove the app data. Deleting a meeting in Gappd removes its local summary, transcript, segments, and associated audio files. Copies in device backups are controlled by your backup provider and settings.

9. Security

Gappd limits processing to the permissions and providers needed for selected features. You are responsible for protecting access to your Mac, local backups, and any optional provider account.

10. Your choices and requests

You can stop recording, delete meetings, change the summary provider, revoke macOS permissions, or uninstall Gappd. Contact leasemobil@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

11. Children

Gappd is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when Gappd’s data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be disclosed before new data use begins.
