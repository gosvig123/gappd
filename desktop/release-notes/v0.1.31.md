- **Person page:** see a person's next Calendar events, their action items, and your Meetings with them.
- **Name speakers:** a queue of unnamed speakers from the last 30 days, so you can name them in one place.
- **Review merge** opens in the suggestion card, and a merge keeps the Person with an email or a real name.
- **Security:** the app blocks pop-up windows and navigation away from Gappd, and turns off Electron features that other programs could misuse.
- Cloud sync records your consent when you turn it on, and a second Mac keeps syncing.
