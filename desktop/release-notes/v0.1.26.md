- **Recording tab replaces Transcript.** When a Meeting has a Screen video, the tab shows the video, a timeline with speaker turns, and a transcript that follows playback. Without a Screen video, it shows the transcript.
- The transcript stops following playback as soon as you scroll.
- Recording notes open from an icon, and the people in the Meeting show in the side column, so the video stays usable in a small window.
- The Meetings header is simpler: calendars refresh on their own. **Settings › Connections** keeps **Refresh** for each account.
