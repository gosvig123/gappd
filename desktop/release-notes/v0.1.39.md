- **About each Person:** after the notes of a Meeting are ready, Gappd notes what each labeled Person said about themselves: work, personal life, preferences, interests and goals. Each fact links to its Meeting and the quote it comes from. The Person page opens with a short summary. **Hide** removes a fact you do not want kept, also from the summary.
- **Build profile from earlier Meetings:** Gappd reads Meetings from before this version only when you ask, with the button on each Person page. It uses your notes provider.
- **New look:** a calm grey theme and a narrow sidebar with icons. Meetings is now the home page: **Next up** shows your next Calendar event with its people, Agenda and Record.
- The record bar names your audio input, Meeting language and screen video in words.
- A Meeting shows its notes and its transcript in two tabs.
