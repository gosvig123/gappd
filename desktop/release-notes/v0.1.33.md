- **Gappd account first:** new installs start with **Create your free Gappd account**. Sign up with Google or any email address. If sign-in does not finish, you can continue without an account for now. Signing in uploads nothing, and Meeting sync stays off until you turn it on in Settings.
- After you sign in or connect Google in your browser, Gappd comes back to the front, and the browser page tells you to go back to Gappd.
- A Meeting whose transcript failed now says so on both tabs, instead of "Notes are off" and "No transcript yet."
- The "Meeting summary model missing" message now points to **Settings › Meeting processing**, where you can download the model.
- The microphone selector now shows a focus ring when you reach it with the keyboard.
