- **Meeting language:** choose the language of a Meeting in the record bar before you press **Record**. Gappd writes the transcript and notes in that language, for example German. Gappd downloads the speech model for a new language once.
- To change the language of a saved Meeting, choose another language at the bottom of the Meeting. Gappd makes a new transcript from the saved audio, then finds the speakers and writes the notes again. If transcription failed, choose a language there to try again.
- **Record bar:** a smaller bar with icons for the microphone, the language and Screen video. Point at an icon to see its full name.
- **Calendar titles:** a Meeting recorded during a Calendar event takes the event's name and its invitees. The AI title no longer replaces it. When you link an event to a Meeting, the Meeting takes that event's name.
- **Simpler setup:** the first setup asks only what **Record** needs. Gappd asks about notes, AI apps and usage reports later, one at a time, when they first help.
- Buttons and page edges now line up across all pages.
- Speaker suggestions no longer fail when Gappd processes several Meetings at the same time.
