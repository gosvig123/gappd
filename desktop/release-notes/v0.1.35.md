- **AI apps:** in **Settings › Connections › AI apps**, connect Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Codex on this Mac to Gappd. They can then search and read your Meetings, with no cloud connection.
- **Remote access:** in **Settings › Connections › Remote access**, let AI services outside this Mac, such as Claude on the web or on a phone, search and read your Meetings while Gappd is open. It needs your Gappd account.
- When AI apps search your Meetings, a word also finds its other forms ("transcribers" finds "transcribe"), and `-word` leaves out Meetings with that word.
- **People:** a simpler list with search, groups, one-line rows and a letter jump. A Person opens beside the list. The Google contacts import prompt goes away after the first import or **Not now**.
- **Error reports:** when something fails, Gappd sends an anonymous report: the step that failed, the error type and code, the place in Gappd's code, and the app and macOS version. It never sends error text, names, titles, paths, transcripts or notes, and no account or ID. Turn it off in **Settings › General › Error reports**.
