- **Connect an AI:** connect Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Codex from Settings. These apps read Meetings straight from this Mac, without Gappd Cloud.
- You can also connect your Claude or ChatGPT account. Sign in to Gappd and keep this Mac awake with Gappd open. **Add to Claude** fills in Claude's connector form. **Add to ChatGPT** copies the server address and opens ChatGPT. Both buttons turn on Remote access; if another Mac has taken over, choose **Use this Mac** first.
- Transcripts no longer wait until you restart Gappd when the Apple speech model was missing. Gappd downloads it again by itself and then transcribes the waiting Meetings.
- **New signing identity.** Gappd is now signed by kristian gosvig. If you have 0.1.36 or older, Gappd does not update by itself to this version: download it once from [getgappd.com](https://getgappd.com) and replace the old app. macOS then asks again for Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording access. Updates are automatic again after that.
